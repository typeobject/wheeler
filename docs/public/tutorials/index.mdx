# Home Was the Easy Part

*Vela* crossed the Reach, completed her work, and arrived where her pilot intended.
The ship declined to call this a return.

One braking window remained. A broken line of evidence traveled with them, along
with several meanings of *home* that had shared a word until the moment they
became dangerous. Mara could land the ship. Osei could restore what the voyage had
disturbed. Sana could establish what the calibration truly showed. Tala had to
keep each of them from completing the wrong duty perfectly.

Their guide was a gray shipboard recension of the Common Book of Return. Its
binding named no author. One rule survived beneath the hands of earlier crews:

```text
Name no distinction before an experiment requires it.
Retain every name that survives the experiment.
```

The mission account reaches back thirty-seven days through Yard Nine, the Black
Vault, Sable, and the weatherward instrument. It begins when Catenary authorized
one ordinary word to carry more responsibility than it could bear.
