# Home Was the Easy Part

*Vela* has crossed the reach, finished her work, and arrived exactly where her pilot intended. According to the ship, this does not
count as returning.

One braking window remains. So does a broken chain of records, and several incompatible meanings of *home*. Mara can land the ship.
Osei can restore what the voyage disturbed. Sana can say what the calibration truly shows. Tala must keep all three from solving
the wrong problem perfectly.

Their only useful guide is a field manual with no accepted author and a rule written on its flyleaf.

```text
Name no distinction before an experiment requires it.
Retain every name that survives the experiment.
```

Between the failed check and the burn lie thirty-seven days: a field manual, a dark moon, several beautiful mistakes, and the slow
invention of a language precise enough to bring them in.
