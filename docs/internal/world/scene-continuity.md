# Scene continuity for *Home Was the Easy Part*

This sheet governs the immersive grounding pass. It supplements the canon and does
not alter technical continuity.

## Viewpoint and narrative distance

The mission account stays close to Tala. A scene may report what Tala sees,
hears, remembers, infers from familiar behavior, or learns in speech and records.
It does not enter another character's private thought.

The calendar below is private continuity scaffolding and should not appear as a
repeated label in the prose. A scene reveals time through sleep, meals, watches,
light, cooling, transit, and depletion. It reveals place while a character moves,
reaches, braces, looks, or encounters resistance. Orientation must arise from the
ongoing action rather than an establishing inventory.

Technical explanation returns regularly to hands, displays, doors, movement,
elapsed work, and consequences in the occupied place.

## *Vela* layout

*Vela* is a narrow rebuilt survey tender. The forward bridge contains Mara's
navigation console and burn cage on centerline. Tala's systems station is to
starboard, Osei's machinery and restoration station to port, and Sana's folding
evidence desk is aft beside the bridge hatch.

One hatch aft leads to the galley and chart table. Four sleeping berths line the
short central passage beyond it. The machine room occupies the sternward
compartment ahead of drive shielding. The service lock opens from the passage
between the berths and machine room.

Under sustained thrust, the sternward bulkhead is down. In coast, handrails,
straps, and magnetic surfaces replace a reliable floor. Yard or habitat rotation
may supply local gravity while docked.

## Archive route

*Vela* docks on the inhabited rim. The crew walks with the wheel's curve from the
arrival lock into the public return gallery. The evacuation exhibit stands near
the gallery's inward end, where a dark window overlooks the Black Vault. Edrin's
verification room lies beyond the exhibit. The route lets the public doctrine
precede the technical distinction it fails to contain.

## Sable route

*Vela* docks at the shielded spin habitat. The one- and two-qubit enclosures share
a laboratory reached through the frost-lined clean corridor. The common galley
sits one level inward.

A maintenance bridge leaves station spin and crosses open truss to the algorithm
racks. The contract and search modules occupy that rack room. The main-array
gallery lies farther outward on the oldest truss. Pressure stairs descend from
that gallery to the three-deck physical chamber.

Repeated movement along this route should let the place accumulate familiarity:
frost grid, bridge weld, Sable below, old truss, refrigeration in the handrail.
