# Instructions for Returning

```text
FIELD MANUAL 7C
Vessel class: long-range courier
Revision: complete voyage edition
Rule: no word before its evidence
```

No manufacturer admitted to printing the original volume. Its oldest paper came from a mill that had closed before the *Vela* was
laid down, while its newest annotations referred to systems installed during the voyage itself. The binding had been repaired in
three ports with three incompatible ideas about permanence.

On the flyleaf, beneath a list of former owners, someone had written the manual's governing rule.

```text
Name no distinction before an experiment requires it.
Retain every name that survives the experiment.
```

The courier *Vela* leaves the turning habitats of Catenary for an instrument built in the dark orbit of Sable. Its crew carries a
calibration deadline, a field manual with no accepted author, and several incompatible ideas about what it means for a machine or a
person to return. At the Archive, a whole culture has mistaken retention for restoration. At the far instrument, an answer no
larger than three depends on physical systems that cannot be copied, inspected, or reversed like ordinary records.

Thirty-seven days later, home is visible and the braking window is closing. *Vela* is in the right place. Its workspace is not
restored, its result lineage is broken, and the mission checker refuses the burn. Tala can repair the claim only by following every
distinction the voyage forced the crew to earn.

The first chapter opened eleven minutes before a braking burn. The last returned to the same countdown with enough language to
separate arrival, inverse execution, rewind, uncomputation, adjoint restoration, measurement, replay, retry, and proof. It also
carried enough trust among the crew to act before the window closed.

A final note occupied the inside cover. Different ink had amended it throughout the journey, but nobody had crossed out its first
line.

```text
A successful return does not erase the journey.
It restores what was promised, preserves what matters,
and accounts for what remains.
```
